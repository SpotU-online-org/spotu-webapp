PROMPT PARA GENERACION DE LOGO — SpotU

---

Create a modern, minimalist logo for "SpotU", an advertising marketplace platform that connects advertisers with advertising space owners (both physical and digital).

Style:
- Clean, modern, and minimalist design
- Professional yet approachable
- Tech-forward but not cold
- Flat design, no gradients unless very subtle

Concept direction:
- The logo should subtly convey the idea of "connection" or "matching" between two parties
- Consider incorporating a subtle pin/location marker (referencing "Spot") merged with a connection/link symbol
- The "U" in SpotU can be stylized to represent a person, a space, or a connection point
- Think of it as the meeting point between supply and demand

Color palette:
- Primary: Electric Indigo (#4F46E5) — trust, technology, professionalism
- Accent: Coral/Salmon (#F97316) — energy, creativity, advertising vibrancy
- The logo should work in full color, single color (black), and reversed (white)

Typography:
- If text is included, use a clean geometric sans-serif font
- "Spot" in primary color, "U" in accent color (or vice versa)
- The wordmark should be readable at small sizes

Requirements:
- No excessive text — the icon should work standalone
- Must be recognizable at 32x32px (favicon size)
- Professional and trustworthy — this handles business transactions
- Should feel like a modern SaaS/marketplace brand (think Airbnb, Stripe, Linear aesthetic)
- Avoid cliches: no generic handshakes, globes, or megaphones

Variations needed:
1. Full logo (icon + wordmark)
2. Icon only (for favicon, app icon)
3. Horizontal layout
4. Dark background version

Output: Vector format, clean paths, production-ready
